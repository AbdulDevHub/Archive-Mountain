The wind howled outside, rattling the old house. Sarah huddled deeper under her blankets, trying to ignore the creaks and groans of the aging structure. But tonight, the familiar sounds of the old house weren't the most unsettling. It was the silence from downstairs. Her parents, usually a comforting presence, had been gone for hours, leaving Sarah alone in the vast, echoing home. She clutched her teddy bear tighter, its worn fur offering little comfort against the growing unease. The shadows in her room seemed to stretch and writhe, taking on monstrous shapes that danced just at the edge of her vision. Her heart hammered against her ribs, a frantic drumbeat against the oppressive quiet. Every tick of the grandfather clock in the hall sounded like a gunshot in the stillness. She squeezed her eyes shut, trying to will the fear away, but it clung to her like a shroud. The house felt different tonight, charged with an unseen energy, and Sarah was acutely aware of her own smallness in the face of the encroaching darkness.
