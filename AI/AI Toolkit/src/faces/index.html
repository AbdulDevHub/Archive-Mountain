Face Emotions

Turn on your camera and the page finds every face, traces 68 landmarks and guesses the expression, about ten times a second. The models run on your device and no video leaves your browser.

The camera stays off until you start it.

How it works

  • Detection. face-api.js (TensorFlow.js) runs a tiny face detector on each video frame.
  • Landmarks and expressions. Two more small networks place 68 points per face and score seven expressions.
  • Private. The four models (about 7 MB) load from this site once; frames are never uploaded.
