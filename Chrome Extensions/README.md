# Chrome Extensions

- [Gallery Bookmark Viewer](./Gallery%20Bookmark%20Viewer): shows your bookmarks as a visual, customizable gallery.

These extensions have their own repos:

- [Gallery Viewer](https://github.com/AbdulDevHub/Gallery-Viewer): offline-first viewer for large image and video folders
- [Local Video Player](https://github.com/AbdulDevHub/Local-Video-Player): local video player with a 500% volume boost, 16x speed and Picture-in-Picture
- [Linkest](https://github.com/AbdulDevHub/Linkest): save tabs, links and notes with batch operations
- [Hydration Hero](https://github.com/AbdulDevHub/Hydration-Hero): privacy-first water intake tracker
- [Gates of Heaven and Hell](https://github.com/AbdulDevHub/Gates-Of-Heaven-And-Hell): website blocker with no bypass
- [YouTube Watch Marker](https://github.com/AbdulDevHub/Youtube-Watch-Marker): mark YouTube videos as watched or save your place
